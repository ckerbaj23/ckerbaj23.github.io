01 Featured project

Bage Journal

A private trading journal and dashboard for Windows. Drop in your broker's export and it rebuilds every trade, charts your performance, and tells you in plain English what is working and what is costing you money.

Version 24 · Private beta
Bage Journal — Insights

Imports from your broker

Reads exports from Webull, Robinhood, and Tradovate futures, then groups the fills into complete trades.

A daily journal

Plan before the open and review after the close: mood, sleep, bias, a grade for discipline, and the lesson of the day.

Private by design

Everything is stored on your own computer, with an optional PIN lock. Your trades never leave your machine.

Built like real software

Python, SQLite, and JavaScript in a native Windows app with an installer, and an automated test run before every release.

Want to try it? The beta is invite-only and the source is private. Send me a note and tell me what you trade. The screens above use the app's built-in demo trades, not real results.

03 About

Trader. Builder. Operator. I bring the same systems-driven mindset to a chart, a staff schedule, and a codebase.

I am a Finance senior at Bryant University with minors in Artificial Intelligence and Communication, graduating in May 2027. I have been actively trading equities, options, and futures since 2019.

During the day I lead operations at Marshfield Famous Pizza and co-own Artisan Jewelers in Marshfield, MA. In my remaining hours, I ship software — connecting APIs, writing trading scripts, and automating the parts of life that shouldn't require human attention.

My main project right now is Bage Journal: a trading journal I built for myself because I wanted to track the goals, emotions, and mistakes behind my trades, not only the profit and loss. I work with AI every day to build it.
