Bage Journal

A private, local trading journal and dashboard for Windows. Drop in your broker's export and it rebuilds every trade, charts your performance, and gives you a place to write down what you were thinking, so you can learn from your own history.

  • Imports broker exports from Webull, Robinhood, and Tradovate futures, and groups the fills into complete trades
  • Dashboard with equity curve, R multiples, risk, and customizable stat tiles
  • Journaling for goals, emotions, and mistakes on every trade, with strategy tags and session windows
  • Private by design — everything is stored on your own computer
